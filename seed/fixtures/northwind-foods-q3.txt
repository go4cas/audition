Northwind Foods plc — Quarterly summary, Q3 (fictional company, made-up figures)

Northwind Foods makes chilled ready meals and own-label soups for three UK supermarket chains.

Results for the quarter
- Revenue: £182.4m (Q3 last year: £171.0m), up 6.7%.
- Gross margin: 24.1% (last year: 26.3%). Higher chicken and packaging costs were only partly passed on.
- Operating profit: £14.9m (last year: £16.2m).
- Net debt: £96m, 1.9 times EBITDA (covenant limit: 3.0 times).
- Free cash flow: £5.1m, after £9.8m spent on a new soup line in Leeds.
- Dividend: 2.1p per share, unchanged.

What management said
- The new Leeds line starts full production next quarter and should lift soup capacity by 40%.
- The largest customer (41% of revenue) has renewed its contract for three years, with a 1.5% price cut from January.
- Energy costs are fixed until the end of next year.
- A plant-based range launched in two chains; early sales are "ahead of plan" but small (under 2% of revenue).

Market
- Shares trade at 11 times expected earnings, against 14 times for listed peers.
- The dividend yield is 4.8%.
